FAQ

How do I join? Send SOL to the official presale address on this page. Your contribution is detected from on‑chain transfers.
What is pro‑rata (fair allocation)? If total raised exceeds the hardcap, every wallet’s counted amount scales by the same factor. Nobody gets unfair priority.
What happens if the presale is oversubscribed? Your token amount is calculated using the allocation factor. Any excess SOL (if refunds are used) is processed after presale end.
When can I claim? The claim button appears automatically when the presale timer reaches 0. Launch follows ~12–24 hours later.
